Fertiliser and Pesticide Consumption in India: A Statewise Analysis of Trends, Factors and Predictions

M.Sc. Dissertation | Ongoing Research

A statewise analysis of trends, factors and predictions in fertiliser and pesticide consumption across India. The study examines differences in consumption, relationships with rainfall, irrigation and cropping intensity, and different patterns of use across Indian states.

The study also aims to develop and compare predictive models for estimating fertiliser consumption using past fertiliser use, rainfall, gross irrigated area and cropping intensity.
